TOKYO — AUG 29–30

First international male act to headline Tokyo's National Stadium — and the full RUN IT tour map lands

Two nights at Japan National Stadium (MUFG Stadium) made Stray Kids the first international male act to stage a solo concert there. Alongside the milestone, World Tour "RUN IT" now has a confirmed first leg of 9 cities and 18 shows: Nagoya (Sep 5–6) and Osaka (Sep 19–20) domes, Fukuoka (Oct 24), Hong Kong's Kai Tak Stadium (Dec 5), Taipei Dome (Dec 12), Bangkok's IMPACT Arena (Jan 16–17, 2027), and Singapore Indoor Stadium (Mar 6–7, 2027) — with further regions still to be announced.

Summarized from K-Concert Guide by Stray Kids Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

NEW YORK — AUG 21

"This & That" becomes Stray Kids' ninth consecutive Billboard 200 No. 1

The album opened atop the Billboard 200 with 369,000 equivalent album units, making Stray Kids the first act in the chart's history to send its first nine charting albums straight to No. 1 — a run that also ties the group with The Rolling Stones for the second-most No. 1 albums by a group in Billboard 200 history, reached faster than any act since The Beatles.

Summarized from CelebrityAccess by Stray Kids Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — AUG 7

"This & That" video crosses 12.5 million views within hours

The title track's music video exploded past the milestone on release day, capping off a mini-album that Korean press described as the group's most genre-fluid set yet — eight tracks stretching from the pre-release single "RUN IT" to the acoustic-leaning closer "Back Then."

Summarized from JRL Charts by Stray Kids Wire staff.

SEOUL — JUN 21

Group announces "RUN IT," a comeback date, and a new world tour in one drop

A cinematic teaser confirmed the pre-release single "RUN IT" for June 24, a full mini-album to follow in August, and a new global run — later revealed as World Tour "RUN IT" — that would eventually take the group to a stadium stage no overseas male act had headlined solo before.

Summarized from Forbes by Stray Kids Wire staff.

SEOUL — JUL 25–AUG 2

World Tour "RUN IT" opens with five nights at KSPO Dome

The tour's launch leg ran July 25, 26, 29, and August 1–2 in Seoul before heading to Japan, where two nights at Tokyo's MUFG Stadium in late August made Stray Kids the first overseas male act to headline a solo concert at the national stadium.

4.1 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"THIS & THAT doesn't resolve the tension in its own title so much as sit inside it — a group built on genre whiplash finally treating that whiplash as the whole point."

THIS & THAT (2026): eight tracks, one pre-release single already out since June, and not much interest in staying in one lane. The mini-album folds "RUN IT" in alongside the title track and six new cuts, moving between the group's signature high-tension production and looser, more melodic writing than recent eras.

Title track "This & That" doesn't ease into the comeback so much as needle-drop into it, and the video's climb past 12.5 million views within hours suggests the group's core audience showed up on schedule. A Festival Version of the title track closes the tracklist.
